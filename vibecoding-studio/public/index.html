What is vibe coding?

The shift from syntax to intent

Traditional coding means mastering languages, frameworks, and boilerplate. Vibe coding flips that: you describe what you want, iterate in conversation, and let AI handle implementation — then you run and refine on your own machine.

Describe Features, flows, and constraints in plain English
Generate AI writes, edits, and debugs the codebase
Launch Test locally, iterate fast, ship when it feels right

How it works

Three steps from idea to running app

  1. 01

    Prompt your vision

    Tell the AI what to build — pages, logic, styling, edge cases. Be specific about the experience you want.

  2. 02

    Iterate with AI

    Review output, ask for changes, fix bugs through conversation. Use Cursor, Claude, or a vibe coding CLI.

  3. 03

    Run on localhost

    Launch the app on your machine, verify behavior, and keep refining until the vibe matches your vision.
